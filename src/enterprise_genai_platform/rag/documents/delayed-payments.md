id: POL-PAY-001
title: Delayed Faster Payments
version: 1.0
classification: internal-synthetic
allowed_roles: transactions
---
When a Faster Payment is delayed, confirm the transaction status and beneficiary details. A delayed acknowledgement may be monitored for up to two hours before escalation to payment operations. The payment must not be resent automatically because doing so may create a duplicate transfer. Record the investigation evidence and advise the customer that the payment remains under review.
