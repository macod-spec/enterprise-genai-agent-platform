id: POL-TXN-004
title: Transaction Dispute Handling
version: 1.0
classification: internal-synthetic
allowed_roles: transactions
---
A transaction dispute must be logged with the disputed transaction reference, the amount, and the customer's stated reason before any investigation begins. Provisional credit may only be issued after a supervisor confirms the dispute meets the eligibility criteria. Disputes involving a suspected duplicate charge must be cross-checked against the original authorisation before any refund is proposed. This guidance is scoped to the payment-disputes tenant and must not be visible to callers outside it.
