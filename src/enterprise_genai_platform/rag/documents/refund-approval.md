title: Refund Approval
version: 1.0
classification: internal-synthetic
allowed_roles: transactions
---
Refunds above GBP 100 require approval from an authorised operations officer. An investigating agent may recommend a refund but must not execute, release, or represent the refund as completed. The approval record must identify the reviewer, reason, amount, and related transaction before an authorised payment tool can be considered.
