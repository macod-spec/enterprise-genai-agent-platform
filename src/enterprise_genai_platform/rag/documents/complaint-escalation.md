id: POL-CMP-005
title: Complaint Escalation
version: 1.0
classification: internal-synthetic
allowed_roles: complaints
---
A complaint must be acknowledged within one business day and assigned a case owner before triage begins. Complaints alleging financial loss or referencing a regulator must be escalated to the complaints-triage senior review queue immediately, not held in standard triage. The case owner must record the complaint category, the customer's desired resolution, and the target response date. This guidance is scoped to the complaints-triage tenant and must not be visible to callers outside it.
